The Process

From first sketch to final handover.

A clear, disciplined 4-stage path with fixed pricing and zero surprises — so you always know what's happening, what comes next, and exactly where every dollar goes.

Scope & Fixed-Price Quote

We assess your space, define the exact scope, materials, and timeline, and provide a binding fixed-price quote with zero hidden surprises before work begins.

Design, Plans & Permits

Our team develops photorealistic 3D models and architectural drawings, coordinates city approvals and permits, and guides finish selection.

Dedicated Construction

A single accountable in-house crew executes your build with daily site oversight, clean dust-containment, and tight schedule adherence.

Handover & 1-Year Warranty

We conduct a thorough final walkthrough with zero loose ends, hand over all documentation, and back every detail with our 1-year workmanship warranty.

WHO WE ARE

Craftsmanship is in the details you never see.

Sophria Construction is an Ontario and Vancouver-based design-build firm specializing in high-end residential renovations and commercial transformations. We were founded on a simple belief: that a home should be renovated with the same care, transparency and precision with which it will be lived in.

From luxury kitchen remodels and spa bathrooms to custom stonework and home additions, every project is guided by unwavering attention to detail, proactive communication, and an ironclad standard of quality — delivered on time and on budget.
